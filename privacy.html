Privacy policy

Nodal: Brain Puzzles · published by NodalGame · effective 18 August 2026

Nodal is a logic puzzle game. This policy describes what it collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is short because the app collects very little.

You can use Nodal without giving us anything

Signing in is optional. The tutorial, the puzzle editor, Escalation mode and any daily puzzle already downloaded all work signed out. If you never sign in, no account is created and we store nothing about you.

What we collect, and only if you sign in

  • Your Google account email address and user ID. Obtained through Google Sign-In. We use it to identify your account and for nothing else. We never receive your Google password.
  • Your puzzle progress. Which puzzles you have attempted and solved, the solutions you entered, attempt counts, and when. This is what lets your progress follow you to a new device, and what streaks are counted from.
  • Your Escalation scores, so a run can be placed on the leaderboard.

What we do not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • No phone number
  • No payment details. In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or billing information.
  • No record of what you bought. Whether you own Remove ads is checked on your device, directly with Google Play, and is never sent to our servers.

Advertising

Nodal is free and supported by occasional full-screen ads shown between puzzles. Ads are served by Google, which may use your device’s advertising ID to select them and to measure whether they were seen. Google’s handling of that data is covered by how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Android settings.

A one-time in-app purchase removes ads permanently. It unlocks no puzzles or features — every part of the game is available without it.

Who else sees your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. Two things are worth naming plainly:

  • If you play Escalation, your score may be shown to other players on an in-app leaderboard.
  • Google acts as our sign-in provider, payment processor and ad network. Data those services handle is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

Where it is kept and for how long

Your account and progress are stored on servers we operate in Amazon Web Services, in the United States. Everything the app sends travels over an encrypted HTTPS connection. We keep your data until you delete your account, after which it is removed — see below.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete account, twice to confirm. Everything is removed immediately and irreversibly.

Full instructions, including how to request deletion if you have already uninstalled the app, are on the account deletion page.

Children

Nodal is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, we will update the effective date at the top and the change will be visible in this page’s public revision history on GitHub.
