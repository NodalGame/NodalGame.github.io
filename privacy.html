Privacy policy

Nodal · published by Nixotica · effective 19 October 2026

Nodal is a logic puzzle game for Android and for Steam on Windows, Linux and Steam Deck. This policy describes what it collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is short because the game collects very little.

Your Nodal account

An account is what lets your progress, streaks and achievements follow you to another device, and what puts your scores on the Escalation leaderboard. How you get one depends on where you play:

  • On Steam, the game signs you in with the Steam account you are playing on the first time it can reach our servers. There is no separate sign-in step, and no password or email address is asked for. If Steam or our servers can't be reached, the game plays offline.
  • On Android, you sign in with Google Play Games. Signing in is optional: the tutorial, the campaign and any daily puzzle already downloaded all work signed out. If you never sign in, no account is created and we store nothing about you.

What we collect

  • The identity your platform gives us. On Steam, your Steam ID, the number that identifies your Steam account. On Android, your Google Play Games player ID and gamertag. Accounts created by earlier Android versions of Nodal, which signed in with Google, also hold that Google account’s email address and user ID. We never receive your Steam or Google password.
  • Your username, if you choose one. It is shown beside your score on the Escalation leaderboard. Without one, you appear there as “Anonymous”. On Steam, the name box suggests your Steam profile name, but it is only saved if you choose it.
  • Your puzzle progress. Which puzzles you have attempted and solved, the solutions you entered, attempt counts, and when. This is what lets your progress follow you to a new device, and what streaks are counted from.
  • Your achievements, and when you earned each one.
  • Your Escalation scores, so a run can be placed on the leaderboard.
  • Linked accounts. If you join your Steam and Android accounts with a one-time code, we record that they are one Nodal account, and merge their progress and achievements.

What we do not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • No phone number, and no email address except on the older Google sign-in accounts described above
  • No payment details. On Android, in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or billing information. On Steam there is nothing to buy.
  • No record of what you bought. Whether you own Remove ads on Android is checked on your device, directly with Google Play, and is never sent to our servers.

Advertising

On Android, Nodal is free and supported by occasional full-screen ads shown between puzzles. Ads are served by Google, which may use your device’s advertising ID to select them and to measure whether they were seen. Google’s handling of that data is covered by how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Android settings.

A one-time in-app purchase removes ads permanently. It unlocks no puzzles or features — every part of the game is available without it.

The Steam version has no ads.

Who else sees your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. Three things are worth naming plainly:

  • If you play Escalation, your score and username may be shown to other players on an in-app leaderboard. Your Steam ID, Play Games ID and email address are never shown.
  • Achievements you earn are also reported to Steam or to Google Play Games, so they appear on your profile there.
  • Valve runs Steam, and Google runs Play Games, Google Play billing and the Android ad network. Data those services handle is governed by Valve’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Where it is kept and for how long

Your account and progress are stored on servers we operate in Amazon Web Services, in the United States. Everything the game sends travels over an encrypted HTTPS connection. We keep your data until you delete your account, after which it is removed — see below.

Deleting your data

In the game, open More → Settings → Account and press Delete account, then Delete for good to confirm. Everything is removed immediately and irreversibly.

Full instructions, including how to request deletion if you can no longer open the game, are on the account deletion page.

Children

Nodal is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, we will update the effective date at the top and the change will be visible in this page’s public revision history on GitHub.
