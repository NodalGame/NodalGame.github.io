Delete your Nodal account

Nodal · published by Nixotica · last updated 19 October 2026

You can delete your Nodal account and everything attached to it at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

From inside the game

The same on Android and on Steam:

  1. Open Nodal and go to More → Settings → Account.
  2. Press Delete account.
  3. It changes to Delete for good. Press it again to confirm. A single press never deletes anything, and leaving the page without the second press cancels it.

If you can no longer open the game

Email nixotica@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Nodal account, and tell us which account it is:

  • on Steam, a link to your Steam profile;
  • on Android, your Google Play Games gamertag;
  • your Nodal username, if you set one.

If your account is an older one that signed in with Google, send the email from that Google account’s address. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours before deleting it. Requests are actioned within 30 days and normally much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Every record of your puzzle progress — attempts, solutions, completions and streaks
  • The achievements we hold for your account
  • Every sign-in identity linked to the account, and any link between your Steam and Android accounts
  • Your Escalation scores, including any leaderboard entries
  • The account record itself

All of it is removed at once. Nothing is retained afterwards, and there is no grace period during which it could be restored.

What is not ours to delete

Achievements also appear on your Steam or Google Play Games profile. Those copies are held by Valve and by Google, not by us, and deleting your Nodal account does not remove them.

If you bought Remove ads on Android, the record of that purchase is held by Google Play, not by us, and is governed by Google’s privacy policy. Deleting your Nodal account does not cancel or refund a purchase, and it does not remove Google’s record of it.

Your entitlement to an ad-free app lives with your Google Play account rather than your Nodal account, so it survives deleting your Nodal account, reinstalling the app, or moving to a new phone.

Playing without an account

On Android, signing in is optional. The tutorial, the campaign and any daily puzzle already downloaded all work signed out. If you never sign in, no account is created and nothing about you is stored on our servers.

On Steam, the game signs you in automatically with your Steam account. If you delete your account and keep playing, a new, empty account is made the next time the game connects.
